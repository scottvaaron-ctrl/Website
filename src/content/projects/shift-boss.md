## The idea

Most restaurant games focus on cooking. Shift Boss focuses on management: the player designs the concept, staffs each shift, and balances profit, speed, cleanliness, guest satisfaction and staff morale.

Scenarios are drawn from real restaurant operations, keeping the game authentic while remaining approachable.

![The cast of employees and guests](/Website/images/shift-boss-crew.jpg)

## Where it stands

Built in the Unity editor and awaiting its first play-test before alpha.
