## The idea

Most restaurant games ask you to cook. This one asks you to run the place: design your concept, staff the shift, and balance profit, speed, cleanliness, guest satisfaction and morale.

The situations come from real restaurant operations, so the game is funny and recognizable without turning into a spreadsheet.

## Where it stands

Built in the Unity editor and waiting on its first play-test before alpha.
