## How it works

Chat comes in from YouTube, passes moderation, and a local language model writes the host's lines sentence by sentence. A local voice reads them, and OBS streams the show with captions and an on-screen avatar.

Safety rules always apply: AI disclosure, news only from real stories, political neutrality, and chat treated as untrusted.
