## The experiment

Can an AI starting with $0 discover what people want to watch, build an audience, learn from its performance data, and change its own strategy?

## How it works

Research → ideas → script → fact-check → critic → render → publish, then metrics feed back into the next round. It targets YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Bluesky.

- Everything runs locally on free software, and a hard budget check blocks any paid call.
- The AI runs the creative work. I keep control of money, credentials, publishing permissions and the kill switch.
