## The experiment

Can an AI system starting with no budget discover what audiences want to watch, build a following, learn from its own performance data and adjust its strategy?

## How it works

A pipeline runs research, ideas, scripting, a fact-check, a critic pass, rendering and publishing for short videos on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Bluesky. The metrics feed back into the next round.

- Everything runs locally on free software. A hard budget check blocks any call that would cost money.
- The AI runs the creative work. I keep control of money, credentials, publishing permissions and the kill switch.
