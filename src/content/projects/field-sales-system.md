## What I built

- A **Field Manual** for how to prospect and pitch.
- An **Outreach Tracker** for the pipeline.

Together they standardized B2B prospecting, territory coverage and pipeline tracking for outreach to about 100 businesses a week.

The same outreach supported the catering line I launched on ezCater (about $3,000 a week) and the community and B2B fundraising partnerships across Monmouth County.
