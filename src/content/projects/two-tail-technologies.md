## What it is

A shared workspace for machine-learning and modeling projects. Each idea is assessed side by side for how achievable it is and what it's worth before we build.

## On the list

- **Company profitability:** forecast profitability quarters ahead from public SEC filings.
- **Development likelihood:** predict which land parcels will be developed, from public parcel, permit and imagery data.
- **Consultant bot:** an AI consultant that combines industry best practices with a company's own SOPs and KPIs.
- **Stock trader:** a trading model judged honestly against buy-and-hold.

All of these are at the idea stage.
