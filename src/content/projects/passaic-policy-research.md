## The question

Why was the homeless population of Passaic County unable to obtain the state and federal identification required for nearly every other service?

## What we did

We diagnosed the policy failures through stakeholder interviews and research on the people affected. Then we worked with state agencies and a mayoral innovation office on data-driven fixes. I analyzed the data in RStudio and SPSS, drawing on comparative historical and public agency data.

## The result

We presented the findings to decision-makers and to the street-level bureaucrats who carry the policy out, weighing the costs and benefits of each fix. The work earned a Certificate of Recognition for Solving Authentic Complex Problems.
