## The idea

Friend groups ("Crews") share photos and moments from their time together, react and comment, and keep a lasting shared history. The core product is memory preservation.

## Where it stands

- The closed alpha is live: accounts, Crews with invite codes, a Crew feed with nine post types and up to ten photos a post, reactions and comments.
- It installs and signs in on a real iPhone.
- Next: finish the closed Beta 1.0 testing pass, then release.
