## The idea

Everything your group chat forgets, remembered. Friend groups ("Crews") post photos and moments from a night out, react and comment, and keep it all as shared history. The theme is the nights out; the product is the memories.

## Where it stands

- Closed alpha is live: accounts, Crews with invite codes, a Crew feed with nine post types and up to ten photos a post, reactions and comments.
- Installs and signs in on a physical iPhone.
- Next: a closed Beta 1.0 testing pass, then release.
