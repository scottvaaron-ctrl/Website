## The idea

Staffing and prep decisions depend on forecasting demand. This project aims to predict how busy a business will be using only public, legally available data, without access to its internal sales figures.

## Where it stands

Planned. The first case study is a quick-service restaurant on a busy state highway, with forecasts on demand.
