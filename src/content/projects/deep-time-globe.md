## The idea

All of recorded history on a single globe. A time slider shows how borders change over time, layered over real satellite imagery. More detail after the 1870s, and no AI-enhanced imagery.

## The plan

Five phases: the globe with an imagery switch and a historical-border time slider; an events layer; real historical imagery; depth before 1870 from old maps; then custom stitching.

## Where it stands

Phase 1 is built and not yet tested live. It is a personal, non-commercial project.
