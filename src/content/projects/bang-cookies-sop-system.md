## What I built

- **American Dream:** a 60+ document operating system spanning 11 departments, delivered as one complete package.
- **Newark Airport (EWR):** dishwashing and equipment procedures built for a high-constraint location.
- **Every store:** a Point of Sale SOP and a Daily Shift Checklist that standardized open to close.
- **Las Vegas flagship:** a multi-phase SOP system built to double as a franchise-readiness template for future units, plus a hiring playbook for the Las Vegas hospitality labor market.

About 100 SOPs in total, across 3 locations.

## Outcome

Store-level procedures that also serve as the franchise template, so each new unit can open on the same documented system.
