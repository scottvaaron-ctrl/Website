## Version 1 (built for Backhouse Group)

I built it as a Discord-based AI consulting assistant for Backhouse Group. It stores client information in plain language, surfaces reminders, and gives on-demand advice, extending the practice's capacity without adding headcount.

## Next phase (Two Tail Technologies)

My brother and I are developing it into a commercial product through [Two Tail Technologies](https://two-tail-technologies.github.io/Website/). The goal: an agent that combines industry best practices with a company's own SOPs, KPIs and goals, available to employees at every level as an in-house consultant. It is designed to work across industries, not only food service. Its current status is tracked on the [Two Tail Technologies site](https://two-tail-technologies.github.io/Website/projects/consulto-bot.html).
