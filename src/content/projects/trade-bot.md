## The idea

A system that proposes, tests and compares trading strategies, paper trades while it learns, and only moves to real money after it passes every validation gate.

## Where it stands

Versions 1, 2 and 3 each traded paper money. Version 4 is in progress.

## The rule

Hypothetical, backtested or paper results are never presented as real performance.
