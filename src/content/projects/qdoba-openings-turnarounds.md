## What I did

- Opened 4 new restaurant locations, training staff in Toyota Production System and TWI Job Instruction methodology.
- Audited and retrained staff at 20+ underperforming stores across four markets.

## Results

- New stores on average doubled sales within two months of opening.
- Turnaround stores cut labor cost by up to 8% and food cost by up to 7%.

## How

Standardize the work, teach it with job instruction, then audit until the standard holds without me in the building.
